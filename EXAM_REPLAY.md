# Frozen exam replay (legacy 3/10-day + full 15-day)

## Important distinction: legacy runner vs full runner

The **legacy** [src/exam_replay/runner.py](src/exam_replay/runner.py) is
premarket-only and is retained for the earlier audit:

- `_replay_orders` loops from 09:30 to 09:59:30 ET, but only evaluates
  `EXECUTE_ORDER` triggers for initial fills.
- `REQUEST_AI_RECHECK` and `INVALIDATE_PLAN` triggers are not evaluated.
- No intraday AI recheck is called.
- Stops are not simulated; `BROKER_STOP` is rejected and `NONE` is used.
- The AI account packet contains cash/equity/position quantities, but not
  average entry, unrealized P&L, stop distance or intraday current price.
- After 09:59:30 the position is carried, marked at the daily close, and
  processed again only on the next selected day's 09:00 premarket snapshot.

Therefore the old 3-day and 10-day results are **not** full intraday
management backtests.

The **official 15-day run** uses
[src/exam_replay/full.py](src/exam_replay/full.py) and
[src/exam_replay/broker.py](src/exam_replay/broker.py):

- 09:30-10:00 minute loop evaluates `EXECUTE_ORDER`, `REQUEST_AI_RECHECK`,
  `INVALIDATE_PLAN`, and protective stops.
- The actual production `INTRADAY_RECHECK` prompt/schema is called on
  rechecks; the production `POSITION_REVIEW` prompt/schema is wired for
  stop adjustment/removal.
- Account packets include cash, equity, buying power, full position detail
  (average entry, mark, unrealized P&L), open orders and active stops.
- 10:00-16:00 is broker-only replay: no new AI or local orders; DAY limits
  expire and GTC stops may trigger with gap/slippage.
- State (cash, positions, fills, stops, orders) carries across all 15
  contiguous NYSE sessions.

## Isolation contract

The replay is implemented in [src/exam_replay/runner.py](src/exam_replay/runner.py).
It never imports Alpaca, the production `Store`, production research memory,
web search or live quotes. It reads only the frozen `data/exam_v2.duckdb`
point-in-time snapshot at 09:00 ET and uses the isolated
`HistoricalSimBroker`.

For every decision day:

1. Build the 09:00 ET snapshot with `get_v2_snapshot`.
2. Give the model only:
   - the compact 48-symbol scan at 09:00 ET;
   - prior completed daily bars, weekly/monthly aggregates;
   - the latest premarket minute bars allowed by the snapshot;
   - dated EXPLORATORY news/SEC metadata/fundamental facts;
   - isolated simulator cash/positions/open orders.
3. No production research memory, web search, live quote, Alpaca account or
   current news is supplied.
4. The AI plan is parsed by the production strict `UniverseScan`/`PremarketPlan`.
5. Only after 09:30 ET does the simulator use regular-session IEX bars.
6. No future bar is present in any AI input.

`EXPLORATORY` is used because it contains the dated news/fundamental records
requested for this experiment. Its historical content versions are UNKNOWN;
this is recorded in every report.

## Shorts

The simulator permits both `OPEN_LONG` and `OPEN_SHORT`. Historical borrow
locates are not in the frozen dataset, so this experiment uses an explicit
assumption:

`ALL_SYMBOLS_ASSUMED_AVAILABLE_FOR_EXAM`

This is not a claim that the stocks were actually borrowable. No short order
was generated by the models in these runs.

## Caveats

- Current watchlist and strategy were selected after the historical dates
  (selection bias).
- IEX is not consolidated SIP.
- EXPLORATORY news/fundamental content versions are not historically archived;
  availability timestamps are used but exact original text versions are UNKNOWN.
- The model's pretraining may contain knowledge of later events; this cannot be
  proven absent.
- Short borrow availability is assumed, not verified.
- Zero or one closed trade cannot establish strategy effectiveness.
- No live or Paper execution is part of this report.
